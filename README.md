Tfm Cardle is a game where you have 5 guesses to find the hidden card.
Each guess will give hints to help you find the hidden card by telling you if it cost more, less or equal, if it is the right color, the right type of requirement, and if it has the right tags and effects.
Only the project cards from the base game and Prelude 1 are supported.

The "Tags" and "Effects" categories tell you what tags and effects are shared by your card and the hidden card.
A category is correct (green background) if it is exactly the same as the hidden card.
A category is partially correct (orange background) if it shares at least one item with the hidden card.
A category is incorrect (red background) if it shares no item with the hidden card.

All possible effects (separated by commas) are:
gaining MC, losing/paying MC or removing MC to a player, gaining MC production, reducing any MC production (same for all 6 standard resources), placing a special tile, placing a city tile, placing a greenery tile, placing an ocean tile, raising temperature, raising oxygen (without greenery), gaining TR, drawing a card, adding or removing an animal to any card, adding or removing a microbe to any card, permanent cost reductions, and non-negative Victory Points.
Any card with an effect that is not one of these (like discarding a card or adding science resource) has the "Other" effect. Here is the exhaustive list of all 19 cards with that effect:
- Cards that can discard other cards by any means: Mars University, Business Contacts, Invention Contest, Inventor's Guild, Business Network, Search for Life
- Cards that can add or remove any resource that is not microbe or animal: Olympus Conference, Physics Complex, Search for Life (again), Security Fleet, CEO's Favorite Project
- Cards that can reduce the cost of other cards but not permanently: Indentured Workers, Psychrophiles
- Cards that can modify global requirements: Adaptation Technology, Special Design
- Cards that can protect resources from being removed: Protected Habitats, Pets
- Cards with a unique effect: Advanced Alloys, Land Claim, Robotic Workforce (and maybe even Search for Life again for revealing a card to all players)

All effects count. Immediate effects, permanent effects from blue cards, action effects from blue cards, and also the cost of actions from blue cards (Equatorial Magnetizer has the negative power production effect).

Cards can only have an effect once (Research only has 1 card draw effect, Sponsors only has 1 MC production effect).

Negative production effects are the same if they affect you or other players (Food factory and Fish have the same negative plant production effect).

All possible effects of a card are used.
For example, Imported Hydrogen's effects are Ocean, Plant, Microbe and Animal, and Hired Raiders' are MC gain, MC loss, steel gain, and steel loss.

You can switch between Daily and Infinite modes.
Daily mode will give the same hidden card for everyone and resets at midnight (Paris time).
Infinite mode will give a random hidden card and let you start a new game at the end.
