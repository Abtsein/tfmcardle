How to play

Guess a Terraforming Mars card and see how it compares to the hidden card to find it!
You have 5 guesses maximum.
Base + Prelude 1 project cards only.

The "Tags" and "Effects" categories tell you what tags and effects are shared by your card and the hidden card.
A category is correct (green background) if it is exactly the same as the hidden card.
A category is partially correct (orange background) if it shares at least one item with the hidden card.
A category is incorrect (red background) if it shares no item with the hidden card.

All possible effects (separated by commas) are:
gaining MC, losing/paying MC or removing MC to a player, gaining MC production, reducing any MC production (same for all 6 standard resources), placing a special tile, placing a city tile, placing a greenery tile, placing an ocean tile, raising temperature, raising oxygen (without greenery), gaining TR, drawing a card, adding or removing an animal to any card, adding or removing a microbe to any card, permanent cost reductions, and non-negative Victory Points.
Any card with an effect that is not one of these (like discarding a card or adding science resource) has the "Other" effect.
The complete list of effects listed as "Other" is available in the README in the GitHub repository:
https://github.com/Abtsein/tfmcardle

All effects count. Immediate effects, permanent effects from blue cards, action effects from blue cards, and also the cost of actions from blue cards (Equatorial Magnetizer has the negative power production effect).

Cards can only have an effect once (Research only has 1 card draw effect, Sponsors only has 1 MC production effect).

Negative production effects are the same if they affect you or other players (Food factory and Fish have the same negative plant production effect).

All possible effects of a card are used. For example, Imported Hydrogen's effects are Ocean, Plant, Microbe and Animal, and Hired Raiders' are MC gain, MC loss, steel gain, and steel loss.
