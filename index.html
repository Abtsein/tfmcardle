How to play

Guess a Terraforming Mars card and see how it compares to the hidden card to find it! You have 5 guesses maximum.

Base + Prelude 1 project cards only.

The "Tags" and "Effects" categories tell you what tags and effects are shared by your card and the hidden card. A category is correct if it is exactly the same as the hidden card. A category is partially correct if it shares at least one item with the hidden card.

All possible effects are: gaining or removing any standard resource, increasing or decreasing any production of any standard resource, placing a special, city, greenery or ocean tile, raising temperature, raising oxygen, gaining 1 TR, drawing 1 card, adding a microbe or an animal to any card. Any card with an effect that is not one of these will have the "?" effect, for example: Land Claim or Invention Contest.

Effects only count if they are part of the immediate effect or the action of the card. Permanent effects like Arctic Algae or Mars University don't count.

Acquired Company counts as having the MC production effect 3 times. Hackers counts as having the MC production effect 4 times. Cards with variable effects like Cartel or Greenhouses count as having any amount of MC production or plants.

Sponsors and Acquired Company will show all effects as correct if the hidden card is Cartel, Satellites or Toll Station. Insects will show a unique "any Plant production" effect that will be correct if the hidden card has any amount of Plant production.

All possible effects of a card are used. For example, Nitrogen-Rich Asteroid has 4 plant production effects, Imported Hydrogen has plants, microbes and animals, and Hired Raiders has 4 steel and 6 MC.

Robotic Workforce counts as having any amount of MC, 2 steel, 1 titanium, 2 plant, 4 power and 4 heat productions.
